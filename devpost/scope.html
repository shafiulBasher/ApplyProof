Scope · Build With AI: Basics

ApplyProof Approved

Know what's missing before you submit.

The unique kernel

ApplyProof checks the applicant's real files against the official rules, and every finding shows its evidence: the exact rule, a quote, and the source page. When it can't confirm something, it says Needs Verification instead of guessing.

How it fits together

The proof: what "working" looks like

"The user opens ApplyProof, uploads an official application guideline and their application documents, and clicks 'Run Readiness Check.' ApplyProof generates a checklist, identifies missing documents and rule violations, and links each finding to the exact source passage. The user fixes an issue, uploads the corrected document, and reruns the check to see the status change."

Who it's for & why it matters

Who

Anyone preparing an application with official rules and several documents: students (scholarships, master's, fellowships) and job applicants. The proof of concept is built for one person: a student about to submit a scholarship package.

Today they…

compare the guide against their files by hand, maybe with a spreadsheet, and hope nothing slipped through.

Why it matters to me

I'm applying to several programs myself, and I want this for others like me too.

Boundary

Now · in the proof of concept
  • Multiple applications for one user (no login yet), each with its own documents and results
  • PDF upload of one official guideline plus the applicant's documents
  • AI extraction of requirements into a checklist (documents, page and word limits, eligibility, deadlines, formatting)
  • Missing-document detection
  • Page and word limits checked with plain code, not AI
  • Basic eligibility: typed-in GPA, degree, and nationality vs. clearly stated rules; anything ambiguous is marked Needs Verification
  • Evidence for every finding: rule, quote, source page
  • Needs Verification whenever something can't be confirmed
  • Fix-first ordering
  • Re-upload and recheck
  • Status view: Passed / Action Needed / Needs Verification
Later · worth doing, not now
  • Sign-up and login for many applicants: each person gets private applications; built right after the MVP works
  • Official source monitoring: notify when requirements change (a key trust differentiator, but too complex for now)
  • Conflict detection across guide, FAQ, and web page
  • Exportable readiness report
  • Checking for required sections in the CV and essay
  • Deadline tracker, reminders, letter tracker, progress tracker
  • Reusable profile, version comparison, application history
  • Collaboration, counselor dashboard, privacy controls
  • Premium tiers and payments
Cut · with reasons
  • Finding programs, jobs, or scholarships: ApplyProof checks applications, it doesn't find them
  • Essay generation: it checks documents, it doesn't write them
  • Admission prediction: it promises readiness, not outcomes
  • Reading eligibility facts from the CV: too error-prone, so the user types them in
